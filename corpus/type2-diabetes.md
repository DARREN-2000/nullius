## Glycaemic targets

An HbA1c target of 53 mmol/mol, which is 7.0 percent, is appropriate for most adults with type 2 diabetes managed on agents that do not cause hypoglycaemia. Relax the target towards 58 mmol/mol, which is 7.5 percent, in older adults, in those with limited life expectancy, and where hypoglycaemia would be hazardous. An HbA1c that has risen by more than 0.5 percent between consecutive measurements indicates loss of glycaemic control and should trigger review of adherence, intercurrent illness, and treatment intensification.

## Escalation of therapy

Metformin remains first line where renal function permits. Add a sodium-glucose cotransporter 2 inhibitor for patients with chronic kidney disease, established atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease, or heart failure, because these agents reduce both cardiovascular events and the rate of kidney function decline independently of their glucose lowering effect. Review the choice of second agent against renal function, weight, hypoglycaemia risk and patient preference rather than HbA1c alone.

## Renal monitoring in diabetes

Measure urine albumin to creatinine ratio and eGFR at least annually in all adults with type 2 diabetes. Albuminuria above 30 mg/g on two occasions indicates diabetic kidney disease and warrants renin-angiotensin system blockade if blood pressure permits. A rising HbA1c together with a falling eGFR indicates poorly controlled diabetic kidney disease and both should be addressed in the same review.

## Metformin and renal function

Review the metformin dose when eGFR falls below 45 mL/min/1.73m2 and stop metformin when eGFR falls below 30 mL/min/1.73m2. Advise patients to hold metformin during any illness causing dehydration, because reduced renal clearance during acute illness is the dominant driver of lactic acidosis risk.

## Annual review components

The annual review should include HbA1c, eGFR, urine albumin to creatinine ratio, blood pressure, lipid profile, retinal screening, foot assessment and a review of all prescribed medicines against current renal function.
