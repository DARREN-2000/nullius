## Severity thresholds

Mild hyperkalaemia is a serum potassium of 5.5 to 5.9 mmol/L, moderate is 6.0 to 6.4 mmol/L, and severe is 6.5 mmol/L or above. Any potassium of 6.0 mmol/L or above, or any potassium above 5.5 mmol/L with electrocardiographic change, is treated as an emergency. Repeat the sample to exclude pseudohyperkalaemia from haemolysis or a difficult venepuncture, but do not delay treatment of a symptomatic or electrocardiographically abnormal patient while waiting for confirmation.

## Immediate assessment

Obtain a twelve lead electrocardiogram immediately. Peaked T waves, a flattened P wave, a widened QRS complex or a sine wave pattern indicate cardiac membrane instability and require immediate calcium administration for myocardial stabilisation. Assess volume status, urine output, and current medicines, and check bicarbonate, glucose and renal function alongside the repeat potassium.

## Treatment sequence

Stabilise the myocardium first with intravenous calcium gluconate when electrocardiographic changes are present. Shift potassium intracellularly with intravenous insulin and dextrose, adding nebulised salbutamol as an adjunct. Then remove potassium from the body using a potassium binder, a loop diuretic if the patient is volume replete and passing urine, or dialysis when hyperkalaemia is refractory or the patient is oliguric. Recheck potassium within two hours of any shifting therapy, because insulin and salbutamol relocate potassium rather than eliminating it and rebound is expected.

## Medication review

Stop or suspend potassium supplements, potassium-sparing diuretics such as spironolactone, and non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs. Reassess the dose of any angiotensin converting enzyme inhibitor or angiotensin receptor blocker rather than reflexively stopping it permanently. Document which exposure was judged responsible and the plan for rechallenge, because undocumented permanent withdrawal of renin-angiotensin system blockade removes proven long-term benefit.

## Dietary and follow up measures

Provide dietary potassium counselling covering fruit juices, bananas, potatoes, tomatoes and salt substitutes containing potassium chloride. Arrange repeat potassium within three to seven days after discharge from the acute episode and confirm which clinician owns the follow-up result.
